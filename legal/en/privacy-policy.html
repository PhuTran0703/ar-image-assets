Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AR Drawora (“the app”, “we”) handles information when you use the app. By using AR Drawora, you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

Your favorites, finished drawings in My Album, drawing progress, streaks, badges, language and other settings are stored only on your device. We do not operate our own servers, we do not require an account, and we cannot access this data. Deleting the app removes it.

2. Camera, photos and microphone

The camera is used to take photos you want to draw, to show sketches over your paper while you trace, and for AR features. Photos you pick from your library are used only to create your drawing. Your device may ask for microphone access when the camera starts; AR Drawora does not record or store audio. Camera frames and photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded by us. Drawings, photos and videos are saved to your library or shared only when you choose to.

3. Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address and information about ad interactions to show, limit and measure ads. Depending on your region and your choices, ads may be personalized. Where required, we ask for your consent, and on iOS you can choose whether to allow tracking. You can review your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy settings. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Purchases

Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check your subscription status; it receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information, but never your payment card details. RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

5. Content downloads

Sketch images and the catalog are downloaded from GitHub Pages, and Emoji Mix images are loaded from Google servers (gstatic.com). Like any web server, these services may record your IP address and basic technical request information.

6. Notifications

Daily drawing reminders are created on your device and are not sent from a server. You can turn them off in Settings → Daily reminder or in your device settings.

7. Children

AR Drawora is made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your choices

You can allow or deny camera, photo library, microphone, notification and tracking permissions at any time in your device settings. Some features may not work without them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at phuphu6892@gmail.com or use Settings → Feedback in the app.
